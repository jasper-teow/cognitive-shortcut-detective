What is a cognitive shortcut?

A cognitive shortcut is a quick way of judging, remembering, deciding, or interpreting information. Shortcuts can be useful and efficient, but they can also create predictable blind spots.

A single situation may involve more than one codex entry. This tool offers possible matches for reflection; it does not diagnose you or claim certainty.

Important distinction: The codex includes heuristics, biases, effects, fallacies, memory phenomena, and related cognitive patterns. “Cognitive shortcut” is the broad, student-friendly term used here.
